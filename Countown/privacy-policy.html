Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 4, 2026

1. Information We Collect

At Muety™, we respect your privacy and are committed to safeguarding your personal data. When you subscribe to our launch newsletter, register for VIP early access, or contact customer service, we collect your email address and communication preferences.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your email address exclusively to send store updates, launch notifications, exclusive festive discounts, and customer support communications related to Muety handloom collections. We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party marketers.

Newsletter subscription records are securely processed through our dedicated server integration with Google Sheets backend storage. Access to subscriber databases is restricted to authorized Muety personnel only.

You have the right to request access to your data or request removal from our subscription list at any time. To unsubscribe or request data deletion, simply send an email to support@muety.in with the subject line "Unsubscribe".
